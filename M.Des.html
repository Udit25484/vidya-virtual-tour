M.Des


The M.Des. (Master of Design) program offers an interdisciplinary approach to design, combining creativity, innovation, and technical skills. It covers a broad range of design disciplines, including product design, interaction design, graphic design, and user experience. The program encourages students to explore design thinking, problem-solving, and human-centered design methodologies, preparing them to create impactful solutions for complex societal challenges. Students learn how to integrate aesthetics with functionality and gain hands-on experience through workshops, design projects, and collaborations with industries. The M.Des. program is ideal for individuals looking to become leaders in design, offering a platform to refine design skills and contribute meaningfully to the design world.
